Founder of SINC

SINC — Society for Innovation, Network & Collaboration

I founded SINC, a technology-focused community and collaboration ecosystem where ambitious people connect, collaborate, learn, build and grow together. It brings developers, designers, students, founders and innovators together so they can find teammates, learn from one another and turn ideas into real projects.

No one should have to pursue their ambitions alone.
