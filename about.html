Biography

Elango S is a full-stack developer and the founder of SINC (Society for Innovation, Network & Collaboration). Online he goes by Elanxzz, the name behind this website, elanxzz.me, and his Instagram handle @_elanxzz_.

He is studying for a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, at Yenepoya University in Bengaluru. Originally from Erode in Tamil Nadu, he started programming at school with small automation scripts and HTML pages.

Today he builds full-stack web applications with React, Next.js, Node.js and MongoDB, often with the OpenAI API in the loop, and is interested in AI-assisted development and automation.

Founder of SINC

SINC — Society for Innovation, Network & Collaboration is a technology-focused community and collaboration ecosystem where ambitious people connect, collaborate, learn, build and grow together. It brings developers, designers, students, founders and innovators together to find teammates, learn from one another and turn ideas into real projects.

As founder, Elango sets SINC’s vision, product direction and strategy, coordinates the team and makes final product decisions. SINC is incubated at Yenepoya (Deemed-to-be University), Bengaluru Campus.

SINC is a separate community from this personal website. Read Elango’s founder profile on sincommunity.in or follow SINC on Instagram.
